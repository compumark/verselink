# VerseLink 1.5.0 — VerseKit Integration & Telemetry

> **DRAFT — FOR REVIEW.** Not a published release. Release date, final commit, tags and image digest are pending approval.

## Highlights

- Added an optional **VerseKit Org Standard v1 integration** so an unmodified VerseKit desktop client can securely connect to one configured VerseLink group.
- Added OAuth device-code pairing, rotating credentials and an explicit, VerseLink-branded **MobiGlass consent page** with individual permission selection.
- Added group-scoped **VerseKit Orders read** support, including required quantities, delivered amounts and current order status.
- Added opt-in **VerseKit order delivery writes**, with server-side authorization, idempotent retries and concurrency-safe updates to VerseLink's existing delivery records.
- Added opt-in **VerseKit Blueprint read** support, providing blueprint names and authorized holders from existing SCMDB ownership data.
- Added server-side **VerseLink Telemetry integration** for the separately distributed Windows client: device pairing, authentication, revocation, heartbeats and private presence snapshots.
- Added MobiGlass **Telemetry device management** and private presence history, including collapsible daily entries, a 90-day retention policy and owner-controlled deletion.
- Preserved the **v1.4.1 mining-material additions** in Orders: Riccite, Borase, Torite, Tin, Silicon and Quartz, with their respective order-number codes.
- Expanded PostgreSQL integration, authorization, isolation and regression testing.

## Deployment and compatibility

- VerseKit integration remains **disabled by default** and is limited to a single deployment-configured VerseLink group.
- `VERSEKIT_ORDERS_WRITE=true` and `VERSEKIT_BLUEPRINTS_READ=true` opt in to their respective capabilities in DEV or Production; neither is enabled by default.
- VerseKit needs its own stable `VERSEKIT_TOKEN_PEPPER`, `VERSEKIT_GROUP_ID` and public HTTPS `VERSEKIT_PUBLIC_URL` when enabled. Do not reuse the SCMDB sink pepper.
- Existing VerseLink groups, Orders, Material Inventory, Missions and SCMDB connections remain available.
- The VerseLink Telemetry **Windows executable is released separately** (`telemetry-v*`); it is not packaged into the VerseLink server Docker image.

## Known limitations

- **TradeMax #129:** A discrepancy in UEX route-profit pricing has been reported, but the root cause is not confirmed. Projected profits may be overstated. The issue remains open and is **not being investigated or changed** for 1.5.0.
- **VerseKit Blueprints:** The implementation and automated coverage are present, but a final manual check of blueprint holders in the unmodified VerseKit client remains outstanding. Keep `VERSEKIT_BLUEPRINTS_READ=false` in Production until explicitly accepted.
- VerseKit `stock:read`, `stock:write` and `blueprints:write` are not included. Snapshot replacement/synchronization semantics remain future work.
